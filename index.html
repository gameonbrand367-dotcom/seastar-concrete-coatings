05 — The System

Builtin layers.

01Surface PreparationThe slab is ground and prepared. Preparation determines everything that follows.
02RepairCracks and imperfections are addressed before any coating is applied.
03Primer / Base SystemA bonded base coat locks the system to the substrate.
04Decorative LayerFlake or decorative media builds the visual character of the floor.
05Topcoat / Protective FinishA protective topcoat seals the system and sets the final gloss.

Every slab is different. Your system is specified after we see the surface — not before.

07 — Performance

Why ownerscoat the slab.

Durability
A coated slab stands up to vehicles, traffic and daily wear better than bare concrete.
Maintenance
A sealed, continuous surface cleans easily — no dusting, no pore-deep stains.
Transformation
The single fastest visual upgrade to a garage, shop or workspace.
Protection
The coating shields the concrete itself from moisture, oils and surface wear.
Customization
Color, flake blend and gloss level are chosen per project.
Clean finish
Seams, cracks and patchwork disappear under a continuous surface.
